# Switchyard

An arcade of original four-direction rhythm routes.

## Language

**Route**: A song with its three charts. _Avoid_: level.

**Chart**: A timed sequence of directional heads and optional hold tails.

**Head**: The moment a lane must first be pressed.

**Tail**: The end of a sustained note, reached while still pressing its lane.

**Chord**: Two heads arriving together; each lane is judged separately.

**Run**: One attempt at a selected route and chart.

**Signal**: The timing judgment for a head or tail.
