# Switchyard specification

Delegated design decisions, not an interactive interview. Design alternatives considered: neon club, celestial dance, rail signal arcade, botanical percussion, monochrome metronome. Selected rail signals plus warm print typography: Switchyard. Original Web Audio arrangements and deterministic charts; no external art or recordings.

Three 32-beat tracks at 108, 124, 140 BPM. Three levels: Local (quarter notes), Express (eighth-note bursts, holds, two-note chords), Night Shift (denser syncopation, holds, two-note chords). No same-lane overlap, maximum two simultaneous lanes. All tracks unlocked; records encourage mastery. Notes rise into four arrow receptors. Hit keys arrows or D/F/J/K or touch pads. Tail must be held to within 90 ms; early release breaks hold. Judgment windows 45/90/140 ms; late heads miss beyond 140 ms. Each head and hold tail earns up to 1000 points. Chords judge lanes independently. Ghost presses are ignored, repeats and multiple sources cannot double-score. Combo breaks on misses; energy starts at 100, misses cost 8, successful units restore 2; zero energy fails. Grade from earned/max points.

Use AudioContext.currentTime for input/render/scheduled sound; synthesized track buffer starts at a known audio-clock epoch with 2-second lead-in. Resume creates a new source from preserved offset. Run generation invalidates async activation so pause/restart/menu cannot overwrite newer runs. Hidden/blurred pages auto-pause; held keys clear. Save validated records/settings and gracefully surface unavailable storage.

Calibration: 12 clock-scheduled clicks; each click can contribute at most one tap, skipped clicks stay skipped. Require 6 taps; median offset clamped ±200 ms; repeated keys ignored. Positive offset means a late input is shifted earlier. Settings: volume, scroll lead, offset, reduced motion. Tutorial explains controls, holds, chords, pause and limitations. Results show score, accuracy, combo, judgment counts and grade.

Test seams delegated: public pure chart, scoring engine, calibration, lifecycle token and save adapter; real browser visible controls for gameplay. Test numerical boundaries, holds/chords, duplicate input, skipping calibration beats, stale async activation, malformed storage. No physical latency, keyboard rollover, dance-pad or audio listening claims from automated tests.
